What a seal shows

  • The stored execution record has not been altered or modified since execution.
  • The referee verdict and score were computed from these exact raw steps.
  • The evidence packet can be independently verified with standard SHA-256 tools.

What it does not show

  • That the agent behaved optimally or achieved business goals.
  • That the record is tamper-proof against unrecorded out-of-band state.
  • Public-key identity signatures (PKI) without an authority.
