What this proves

What it does not prove

Exponential Jittered Backoff for Rate Limits

Diagnosis: Agent attempted immediate retries without respecting HTTP 429 Retry-After directives from the upstream payment proxy.

Remediation: Configure retry decorators with full-jitter exponential backoff respecting Retry-After response headers. 

--- a/app/subject.py
+++ b/app/subject.py
@@ -18,3 +18,7 @@
-for attempt in range(max_retries):
-    res = await call_api()
+backoff = RetryPolicy(
+    backoff_strategy="exponential_with_jitter",
+    respect_retry_after=True,
+    max_delay=30.0
+)
